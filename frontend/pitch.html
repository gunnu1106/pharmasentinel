The Solution

PharmaSentinel does three things

👂

Listen

Continuously crawl Twitter/X, Reddit, Quora & health forums for drug-related patient reports

🔍

Compare

Cross-reference every detected side effect against the CDSCO-approved drug label — flag what's unlisted

📋

Report

Score signals using PRR and auto-generate CDSCO-format pharmacovigilance reports ready for submission

Key insight: Most social listening tools tell you what people are saying. We tell you what people are saying that regulators don't know about yet.

NLP Pipeline

Filters first-person reports ("I", "my", "I've") from general discussion. Detects negations. Extracts ADR terms from 15 medical categories.

PII Protection

Detects names, phones, Aadhaar, email, Reddit usernames. SHA-256 hashes author IDs. GDPR & health data compliant.

4 / 9
Signal Science

PRR — the same method used by WHO & EMA

PRR = (n_drug+ADR / n_drug_total) ÷ (n_background+ADR / n_background_total)
≥ 2.0
PRR threshold for signal
≥ 3
Minimum independent reports
≥ 4.0
Chi-squared significance

Real example detected by PharmaSentinel:

Drug: Azithromycin  ·  ADR: Tinnitus  ·  Reports: 3 independent  ·  PRR: 71.4  ·  CDSCO Label: NOT LISTED  ·  Priority: HIGH 🚨
